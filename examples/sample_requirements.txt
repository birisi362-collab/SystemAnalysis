SYSTEM: Integrated Mission Management System

3.1 The system shall consist of a Mission Computer, Radar Unit,
Communication Unit and Power Supply Unit.

3.2 The Radar Unit shall provide target detection information to the
Mission Computer.

3.3 The Mission Computer shall process target information and send
mission data to the Communication Unit.

3.4 The Power Supply Unit shall provide electrical power to the Radar Unit,
Mission Computer and Communication Unit.

3.5 The Communication Unit shall exchange mission data with an external
Command and Control System.

3.6 The Radar Unit shall communicate with the Mission Computer over Ethernet.
The interface protocol between the Mission Computer and Communication Unit
is to be defined by the detailed interface specification.
